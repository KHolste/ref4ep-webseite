Software

Tools that emerge from the Ref4EP context: simulation codes, analysis programs and utilities for work on electric space propulsion. This section grows with the project - further programs will be added as soon as they reach a state in which they are useful beyond our own research group.

Available programs

Each program has its own page with a description, figures from actual use, system requirements and download.

Simulation

IONTRACE

Simulation code for ion optics and extraction from gridded ion sources, either fully three-dimensional or translationally symmetric in two dimensions. IONTRACE computes electric fields and ion trajectories for static or time-dependent electrode potentials, taking into account space charge, neutral gas background and charge-exchange collisions self-consistently. Electrode erosion and the resulting lifetime can also be investigated.

Simulation

FORCETRACE

Simulation program for the effect of keV ion beams on bodies and vacuum facilities: force and torque on bodies at arbitrary inclination, heat load and sputtering, measurement planning on the thrust balance and the motion of an object in the beam of an Ion Beam Shepherd. Within the facility, FORCETRACE computes the neutral gas density, gauge readings, and charge exchange and scattering of the beam up to the Faraday cups.

Windows (64-bit) Version 0.9 Access on request

Simulation

Ion–Surface Interaction

Simulation program for the behaviour of ions at solid surfaces: reflection and backscattering of the projectiles, sputtering of the target, and the charge state of the returning and the sputtered particles. The collision kinematics are computed by the binary collision code RustBCA; charge exchange at the surface is described by a dedicated three-channel model. The target range is low-energy interaction from about 1 eV up into the keV range.
