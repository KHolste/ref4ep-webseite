2. Purpose of the website

This website provides information about the DLR-funded research project Ref4EP2 and its predecessor project Ref4EP. It serves to present the project's objectives, content and scientific and technical information about the project to the public.

3. Access data and server log files

When this website is accessed, the web server processes access data that is technically necessary. This may include, in particular, the IP address, date and time of access, the page requested, the amount of data transferred, browser type, operating system and referrer URL.

This data is processed to deliver the website technically, to ensure system security and for error analysis. This data is not combined with other data sources.

4. Cookies, tracking and external services

As things currently stand, this website does not use any tracking or analytics tools, externally embedded map or video services, or marketing cookies.

5. Contacting us

If you contact us by email, the information you provide will be processed in order to handle your enquiry. It will not be passed on to third parties unless this is necessary to handle the enquiry or there is a legal obligation to do so.

6. Rights of data subjects

In accordance with the statutory provisions, data subjects have in particular the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection to processing and data portability.

7. Right to lodge a complaint

Data subjects have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority if they consider that the processing of their personal data infringes data protection law.

8. Project portal (portal.ref4ep.de)

At portal.ref4ep.de, the controller named in Section 1 operates an internal portal through which the partners of the project consortium collaborate (work packages, milestones, meetings, test campaigns, project documents). Access is restricted to persons for whom the portal administration has created an account; self-registration is not possible. Only the login page and the download library of released documents are publicly accessible without logging in.

8.1 Account and contact data

For each account, the name, email address, partner institution, role in the portal and assignment to work packages are stored. The password is not stored in plain text, but only as a cryptographic hash (Argon2).

The portal's contact directory additionally contains contact persons at the partner institutions with name, position, email address and telephone number, where provided. This information is only visible to logged-in users.

8.2 Collaboration content

Content created in the portal – documents and their versions, comments, meeting minutes with participants, decisions and tasks, test campaigns with participants, notes, photos and file attachments – is stored together with the person who created or uploaded it and the time at which this happened. It is visible to logged-in members of the consortium; individual documents are visible only to the members of the respective work package. Documents that have been expressly released for publication appear in the public download library.

8.3 Change log

Every modifying action in the portal (for example creating, editing, releasing or deleting) is logged with the time, the person acting, the type of change, the record concerned and the IP address. The log serves to make the project work traceable and to keep the system secure; it can only be viewed by the portal administration. The IP address is automatically removed from the log after 90 days; the remaining information is retained in the same way as the project data (Section 8.6).

8.4 Cookies and browser storage

After login, the portal sets two cookies that are technically necessary for its operation and therefore do not require consent (Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG):

  • ref4ep_session maintains the login (valid for 7 days or until logout),
  • ref4ep_csrf protects forms against forgery by third-party websites (same lifetime).

In addition, the portal stores in your browser's local storage your choice between light and dark appearance, for administrators the selected view, and the time of your last visit in order to highlight new entries. This information remains in your browser and is not transmitted to the server. No tracking or analytics tools or external services are used in the portal either; fonts are loaded from our own server.

8.5 Server logs and data backup

As with the website (Section 3), the web server processes access data, including the IP address, when the portal is accessed; these logs are deleted after 14 days. The portal's application log, which also contains IP addresses, is deleted after 14 days.

To protect against data loss, the entire portal database is backed up daily. Backups are automatically deleted after 14 days.

8.6 Legal basis, recipients and storage period

The legal basis for processing in the portal, including the logs, is Art. 6(1)(e) GDPR in conjunction with Section 3(1) of the Hessian Data Protection and Freedom of Information Act (HDSIG): the processing is necessary to carry out the publicly funded research project and to operate the platform used for it securely.

The data is only accessible to logged-in members of the consortium within the scope of their permissions and is not passed on to third parties unless there is a legal obligation to do so. The server on which the portal and website are operated is hosted by IONOS SE, Elgendorfer Str. 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany; IONOS processes the data as a processor within the meaning of Art. 28 GDPR.

Account and project data are stored for the duration of the project. If a person leaves the project, their account is deactivated; on request, their account data is deleted insofar as it is not required to keep the project work traceable. After the end of the project, all accounts are deleted. The project records are archived for ten years in accordance with the rules of good scientific practice and deleted thereafter. For access, rectification or erasure, please use the contact address given in Section 1.

This privacy policy may be amended if technical, organisational or legal conditions change.
