Ref4EP: Comparable Testing for Electric Space Propulsion

Ref4EP develops reference sources, diagnostics and data formats so that electric space propulsion systems can be tested more reliably, more reproducibly and comparably across different facilities.

Why Ref4EP?

Electric space propulsion is a key technology for modern satellite missions. To assess its performance on the ground with confidence, measurement methods, test environments and diagnostics must become comparable and traceable.

Challenge

Understanding facility effects

Ground test facilities differ from space. Background pressure, wall interactions, electrical boundary conditions and neutral gas distributions can affect measured data.

Approach

Creating reference systems

Ref4EP uses reference sources and standardised diagnostics to compare measurement methods, build calibration chains and prepare round-robin tests between test facilities.

Vision

Establishing open standards

Technical documentation, measurement data, models and good practices are to be made accessible in a structured way, supporting their reuse by research, industry and public agencies.

Objectives of the project family

Ref4EP lays the foundations for reference diagnostics in electric space propulsion. Ref4EP2 extends this approach with systematic round-robin tests, long-term validation, facility models and the integration of a reference Hall thruster.

Ref4EP
Foundations for reference diagnostics
Construction of a reference gridded ion source, development of RPA and Faraday reference diagnostics, establishment of an energy standard and preparation of round-robin tests.
Ref4EP2
Extension and validation
Long-term investigations, a standardised reference test environment, model-based assessment of facility effects and comparison of ion and Hall thrusters.

Consortium partners

The Ref4EP consortium combines expertise in electric space propulsion, surface and plasma diagnostics, space electronics, test facility infrastructure and Hall thruster development.

JLU

Justus Liebig University Giessen

Coordination, calibration stand, energy diagnostics, facility effects and test facility characterisation.

IOM

Leibniz Institute of Surface Engineering (IOM), Leipzig

Reference gridded ion source, Faraday sensors, LIF/TALIF and optical validation.

CAU

Kiel University

Thrust, thermal, RPA, E×B and CDA diagnostics for independent validation.

THM

Technische Hochschule Mittelhessen (University of Applied Sciences)

RF generators, electronics platforms, probe electronics and EMC-oriented system integration.

TUD

TU Dresden

Development, construction and characterisation of a reference Hall thruster in Ref4EP2.

Work areas

The work ranges from concrete hardware development to research data management. The key is the combination of reference hardware, diagnostic methods and reproducible procedures.

Reference gridded ion source

Development, operation, long-term validation and provision of identical sources for round-robin tests.

Energy and current diagnostics

Reference RPA, Faraday sensors, calibration stand and independent comparison diagnostics.

Facility effects

Systematic characterisation of wall, pressure, neutral gas and current path effects in test facilities.

Reference Hall thruster

Complementary reference source for open discharge systems and validation across different thruster types.

Electronics and data acquisition

Interference-resistant measurement electronics, RF and power electronics, and modular platforms for diagnostic systems.

Round-robin tests and good practices

Comparative measurements between test facilities and derivation of traceable measurement and analysis procedures.

Open data, open hardware, traceable standards

Ref4EP aims to prepare reference hardware, technical documentation, measurement data, models, analysis tools and measurement procedures so that they remain traceable and reusable in the long term.
